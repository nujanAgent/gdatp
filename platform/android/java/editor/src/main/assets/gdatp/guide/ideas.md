# Inventing a game idea that is not the first thing anyone would make

The first idea is what everyone has. Write three, and keep going until one surprises you.

**Start from a verb, not a theme.** A theme can be swapped in a minute; a verb is the game. Good verbs are specific: herd, splice, swap, bounce, trade, tilt, rewind, grow, reflect, bribe, sneak, stack, untangle, sling, paint, tow, echo, freeze, feed, rotate the world, draw the path, shrink.

**Ways to get something new from something known:**
- **Swap who you are.** You are the dungeon, not the hero; the last tower, not the army; the getaway driver's map reader.
- **Remove the obvious thing.** A platformer with no jump; a shooter where you cannot aim; a racer with no brake; a puzzle with no undo except time.
- **Join two verbs.** Tower defense plus rhythm; match-three plus fighting; golf plus dungeon; cooking plus bullet hell. Ask what one rule makes them need each other.
- **One rule that changes everything.** Time moves only when you move. Gravity follows your last wall. Every enemy you beat becomes your next weapon. The level is what you drew.
- **A constraint.** One screen. One button. Ten seconds a level. Everything is a circle. The whole game happens in a lift.
- **A real thing nobody made a game of.** Sorting mail, tuning a radio, mending a net, herding geese, parking a ferry.

**Test each idea:**
- Say it without naming a genre or a theme. If what is left fits a thousand games, it is not there yet.
- What decision does the player make every few seconds? "Press at the right time" alone is thin; "press now for less, or wait for more and risk it" is a game.
- What does the tenth minute have that the first did not? If nothing, what new rule or pressure could arrive?
- Can it be finished here, with drawn or fetched art, on a phone? Choose a smaller idea done fully over a big one done thinly.

**When the person named a game or a genre,** keep it and make it the real thing: study it, build what actually makes it work, then add one thing of your own. Do not replace it with an easier game wearing its name.

**Sameness to avoid when nothing was asked for:** a character running right collecting coins past spikes; floating islands; gems and a portal; blue sky and green grass; a slime as the enemy; three lives and a score at the top left. Use one only when it is the point.
